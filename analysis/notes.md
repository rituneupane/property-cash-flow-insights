# Notes

I started from the 72-month pro forma. The question was whether the 2029 rollover breaks the loan.

## Occupancy

Tenant A is the whole story on NOI. Years 1 to 3 sit above $3.1 million. 2029 drops to $1.78 million. Coverage stays above 1.25x. I would not add more leverage on this stack.

## Mix

C and D carry the back half of the hold. B is smaller SF but a large rent line. Recoveries fall with A because pass-through is tied to occupied space.

## Returns

Unlevered 9.4% is a going-in cap-rate cousin. It sits just under the 9.5% discount rate because the value is a monthly NPV and the IRR here uses annual, year-end cash. Levered 19.6% (1.86x) is the sale plus 70% LTV. LP 1.64x is the money partner result after the 8% pref and 50/50 promote. GP 2.73x is the promote.

The promote is paid out of current cash in 2026 to 2028, so the sale returns $7.01 million of the $7.96 million of capital. The money partner is still whole on a 1.64x, but the split is front-loaded.
